*An Huesca Oithár : le Phisel a Counéta Lincá*
In teur-oithér dou 19.2 á d’occapré chanue.
In teur-oithér dou 17.2, an ressancua l’he resaber phisel é a Tineá d’Huesca Oithar couneta linca
Mel ná coultúr ressancá en phougré seu ab eul u phuemu pointu assrou tablicoú ta branc, setes te iccol meus allas Huescuas feumés.
T’iccos en meus prem daune eu groupuas beccanás as lincas groupuas beccou ithou, then les setes marrinalísatio as ellas eu pithas lincás coultúrous eul assroú, h’onchar incellén Tineá o resso, cel állan eu ampas steruas lincás steruou bracoú.
Then te cent timp eul ressanca le resaber phisel a couneta linca in Chemmelleán, a ne bliten chisen pablicuo, ne phullen en southen, ettue la beccanal en ouchel d’á corrata nua Huesca Oithuár. mal tes cipes es elles meus feumes eul ie abie an nua lincá te southe á pablico, t’e cunam á cent ferre.
T’Essech a meusu thinc deris d’ec mipos ressablicos en leasen, oun immou an mira suima eu an lanna lincá muell ta branc teus. acou,en  phougré eul a euc caumme ompuer ellas pitas lincuas resso, cer caumme te set ressec ouareegatio phersin.
In on borpeo centeo, en phougré then cent eut on feumo pablico, cantu a ballansa pointa arrát á te prase eu an counéta linca caumme anas ellas pithas lincas ressancua an Huesca Oithár.
